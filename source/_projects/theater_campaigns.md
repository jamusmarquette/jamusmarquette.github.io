  - >
    Theater posters promoting a variety of performances, designed to work as flexible systems across a variety of formats.

  - >
    Underlying concepts unique to each performance were defined through script studies, attendance of play readings, and consultation with directors, playwrights, and marketing teams, to create original artwork that charms theater enthusiasts into the seats of a variety of Off-Broadway playhouses.
