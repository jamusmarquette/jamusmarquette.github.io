description:
  - >
    Restaurants on Mission (RE) is a foundation that aims to provide a step towards a better life for people completing reformation and reintegration programs through nonprofit restaurants, and create a viable impact on families and local communities.

  - >
    The identity system and various assets were created, in addition to a digital campaign for the launch of their first restaurant in Medellin, Colombia.

  - >
    The logo composed of the moniker “RE”—a prefix, meaning again—is set in a forward rising arrangement that serves as the catalyst of a visual language symbolizing the life changing step towards a better life provided by Restaurants on Mission, as well as its expansive community impact.
